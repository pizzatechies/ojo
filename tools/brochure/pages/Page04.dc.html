OJO Sentinel
Core capabilities
03 Inventory & money
Inventory intelligence
Sensors count stock on their own. When a storekeeper’s figures don’t match, you’re told directly.
Goods tamper alerts
Seal and weight checked at every checkpoint, from origin to delivery.
Money tracking
Cash drawer checks, refund and void patterns, and payouts to unknown accounts.
04 People & movement
Satellite & GPS tracking
Vehicles, cargo and key personnel, with route-corridor alerts.
Employee geo-positioning
Who is on site, when they arrived, and time away during a shift.
Phone location checks
Spots faked GPS and impossible travel on driver and staff phones.
05 AI threat detection
Behavioural analysis
Learns each person’s normal, and flags what isn’t.
Coordinated theft
Joins weak signals across people and places into one finding.
Continuous learning
Every alert you confirm or dismiss makes the next one sharper.
One system, one screen
All of it arrives on one owner dashboard, with a live incident feed to your phone.
When something serious happens, you get the alert, the camera feed and a one-tap lockdown.
