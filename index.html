Why OJO Sentinel exists

In the noise, things go wrong quietly.

You are running a hundred things at once. By the time you notice a loss, it has usually been going on for weeks. OJO Sentinel was built for the business owner who deserves to know the truth about what is happening in their business, in real time, without filters.

  • StockroomStock walks out the door, and the storekeeper's count still says everything is there.
  • RecordsFigures are adjusted and written off, and the books still balance.
  • In transitDeliveries are opened on the road and resealed before they reach you.
  • The tillRefunds, voids and "discounts" pile up for one cashier.
  • InboxA convincing email asks finance to pay a "new" supplier account.
  • TogetherSmall signals across several people add up to coordinated theft.

What OJO Sentinel covers that others miss

Most accounting software handles what you put into it. OJO Sentinel checks your operations against your books, and your books against KRA.

  • A storekeeper reports 200 units sold, but the till counted 240. The 40-unit gap is flagged as missing cash and undeclared VAT at the same time.
  • A supplier's invoice arrives but their KRA PIN is inactive. Payment is held before it becomes a disallowed expense.
  • Sales grew this month but the VAT return didn't. The gap between your books and your filing is flagged as a compliance exposure.
  • One branch is losing money while the others make a profit. It's identified, with its cost structure, for your review.
  • PAYE was missed by three days. The penalty is logged and the interest is calculated daily until it's paid.
  • A county permit expired eight days ago. You were warned for 60 days, and the breach is on record until it's renewed.
