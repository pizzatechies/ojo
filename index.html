Why OJO Sentinel exists

In the noise, things go wrong quietly.

You are running a hundred things at once. By the time you notice a loss, it has usually been going on for weeks. OJO Sentinel was built for the business owner who deserves to know the truth about what is happening in their business, in real time, without filters.

  • StockroomStock walks out the door, and the storekeeper's count still says everything is there.
  • RecordsFigures are adjusted and written off, and the books still balance.
  • In transitDeliveries are opened on the road and resealed before they reach you.
  • The tillRefunds, voids and "discounts" pile up for one cashier.
  • InboxA convincing email asks finance to pay a "new" supplier account.
  • TogetherSmall signals across several people add up to coordinated theft.

Core capabilities

Five pillars. One command dashboard.

Physical security, digital protection, inventory control, people tracking and AI threat detection, built as one system that sees across all of them.

01

Physical security

  • Smart CCTV BrainConnects to the cameras you already own. No rip-and-replace.
  • Facial recognitionKnows your staff, and flags known offenders on entry.
  • Restricted area alertsAnyone without clearance in a stockroom or cash office is escalated instantly.
  • Weapons detectionAlerts security at once, and can lock the building down automatically.
  • Day & night modulesAt night, any unrecognised presence is an intrusion.
02

Digital & cyber

  • Fake email detectionCatches look-alike domains, impersonated executives and "new bank details" scams.
  • Account protectionSpots password guessing and credential stuffing, and blocks the attacker.
  • Honeypot trapsDecoy logins, keys and files that expose hackers and insiders who take the bait.
03

Inventory & money

  • Inventory intelligenceSensors count stock on their own. When a storekeeper's figures don't match, you're told directly.
  • Goods tamper alertsSeal and weight checked at every checkpoint, from origin to delivery.
  • Money trackingCash drawer checks, refund and void patterns, and payouts to unknown accounts.
04

People & movement

  • Satellite & GPS trackingVehicles, cargo and key personnel, with route-corridor alerts.
  • Employee geo-positioningWho is on site, when they arrived, and time away during a shift.
  • Phone location checksSpots faked GPS and impossible travel on driver and staff phones.
05

AI threat detection

  • Behavioural analysisLearns each person's normal, and flags what isn't.
  • Coordinated theftJoins weak signals across people and places into one finding.
  • Continuous learningEvery alert you confirm or dismiss makes the next one sharper.

All of it arrives on one unified owner dashboard, with a live incident feed to your phone. When something serious happens, you get the alert, the camera feed and a one-tap lockdown.

Our uncompromising principle

No one can hide the truth from you. Not even your managers.

Owner-direct alerts

Theft, tampering, cash and security alerts go straight to you and OJO Stores Management. Only you can close them.

No override for managers

A manager who tries to dismiss an alert, release tampered goods or lift a lockdown is refused, and you are told who tried.

A record that can't be rewritten

Every sensitive action is written to a hash-chained ledger. If anyone edits or deletes history, the chain breaks and the dashboard shows it.
